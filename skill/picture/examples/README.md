# Example metaphors

Static SVGs from the 2026-10-24 spike, kept as the register to copy. Numbers are approximate and illustrative.

| File | Question (verbatim) | Shape | Metaphor | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `jars.svg` | "so how many hours is 3 weeks at 20 hours a week, picture it" | quantity | jars filling with hour blocks | one jar per week, one block per 5 hours; three full jars hold twelve blocks |
| `clinic.svg` | "But wait what difference MoE makes? explain in very simple terms" | mechanism | one doctor vs a clinic | dense = one doctor for every patient; MoE = reception sends you to 2 of 8 rooms |
| `trail.svg` | "so where we at with this project?" | status | a trail with camps | tents behind you are done, the pin is now, dotted camps are open work |

Each is self-contained: literal light-theme colors, no script, a `<style>` block for the two text classes.
